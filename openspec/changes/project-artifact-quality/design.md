# Design

Agent-owned facts curate the project entrance; the renderer keeps seven clear roles and
preserves existing native/human text. Internal facts/mapping records are generation and
coverage metadata, never competing behavior/decision authorities. Status binds inputs to
current evidence and leaves missing project knowledge explicit.

A capability map covers production/config/build files with actual business responsibility,
current requirements, pending scope or justified support exclusions. Partial static analysis
never implies whole-scope completion. Counts and declarations do not certify semantics.

Narrow pointer-only/Depends-On guards catch definite spec defects; semantic review must hide
Baseline, derive concrete assertions from specs, then reopen source to validate/fill detail.
Whole onboarding checks reviewed artifact scope plus three explicit quality topics. Compilation
and early setup/empty-spec reports cannot masquerade as current behavior verification.
